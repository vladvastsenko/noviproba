makeit – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android application makeit (fi.vladi.makeit), developed by Vlad Vastsenko (vladi.fi).

Local projects and images

makeit does not require an account. Images selected by you, drawings, project settings and generated templates are processed locally on your device. The app does not upload these project contents to a developer-operated server. PDF files are saved to a location you choose; if you select a third-party cloud storage provider, its own privacy terms apply.

Advertising and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform to display advertising, manage privacy choices, measure advertising performance and prevent fraud. Google may collect IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and approximate location inferred from IP addresses. Data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners, including outside your country, subject to applicable safeguards. Where required, consent is requested before advertising data processing that requires consent. You may change your choices through Privacy options in the app settings when available. Core editing and PDF features remain available without internet access.

See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Legal bases and retention

Where the GDPR applies, processing for personalized advertising and other purposes requiring consent is based on consent. Necessary security and fraud prevention may rely on legitimate interests where permitted by law. Local project data remains until you delete it or remove the app; exported files may remain in their chosen storage location. Google and its partners retain advertising data under their respective policies.

Your choices and rights

You can delete projects and exported files, revoke applicable advertising consent and manage advertising identifiers through Android settings. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of your data and complain to a supervisory authority. Requests concerning data held by Google may also be directed to Google.

Children

makeit is a general-purpose DIY tool and is not specifically directed at children.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the contact details at vladi.fi. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
