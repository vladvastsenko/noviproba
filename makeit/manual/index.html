About Makeit

Makeit is a free digital workshop for the whole family. Create cutting templates, paint-by-number pictures, full-size color prints and interlocking 3D figures from photos, drawings and ready-made ideas. No registration or subscription is required. Ads may appear in some sections.

02

First launch

Choose one of 14 languages and tap Start. The bottom menu contains Home, Ideas, Create, Projects and Settings. Change the language in Settings. The editors do not need a permanent internet connection.

03

Choose an image

Open Create, choose one of the four modes and select an image from your device. For a ready-made idea, open Ideas, choose a drawing and an available mode. For clean outlines, use sharp images with a simple background and a clearly distinguishable subject.

04

Cutting templates

  1. Select an image in the pattern editor.
  2. Enable background removal if needed. Adjust the four subject-boundary sliders and tolerance until the outline is clean.
  3. Choose cutting outlines or outlines with details. Adjust sensitivity, line width and the exterior-outline option.
  4. Enable line editing to draw missing parts or erase unwanted lines. Undo, redo or clear edits, and add circular or oval holes.
  5. Set width and height in centimeters, locking proportions if needed. Choose A4 or A3 and whether to skip blank sheets. Save the project and PDF.

05

Paint by numbers

  1. Choose Paint by numbers and select a photo.
  2. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard, or adjust the number of colors (4–48), detail and minimum region size. More detail and smaller regions usually make the picture harder.
  3. Tap Create. Check outlines and color view using the zoomable preview. Regenerate after changing parameters.
  4. Set line width, dimensions, A4/A3 and portrait or landscape orientation. For a small picture choose Fit on one sheet.
  5. Save the project and export PDF. It includes numbered regions, a numbered palette with HEX codes, a color reference and calibration square. Match real paints to the reference: screen and print colors may differ.

06

Full-size color printing

Choose Full-size printing and select an image. If needed, enable automatic background removal and check the result. Enter width and height in centimeters and choose A4 or A3. Save the project and export the color PDF. The app divides the image into sheets for assembling a poster, decoration or template.

07

Interlocking 3D figures

Ideas contains a snowman, Christmas tree, rabbit and pumpkin. You can also choose Create → 3D figure and select your own image. The app creates two flat slotted parts that fit together at 90°, not a 3D-printer model.

  1. Choose a figure or your own simple silhouette. Check automatic subject extraction.
  2. Enter width and height in centimeters, actual material thickness and clearance in millimeters. Slot width is thickness plus clearance.
  3. Choose A4/A3, check parts A and B, and save the project and PDF.
  4. Print, assemble the sheets, transfer the outlines to cardboard, plywood or suitable material and cut the parts. Test the slot on scrap first. Join the parts at 90°.

09

Projects and storage

Tap Save project in an editor to resume later. Open saved work from Projects. Cutting templates can be renamed, duplicated or deleted. Projects are stored locally; automatic cloud synchronization is not provided. Keep original images and finished PDFs separately, especially before uninstalling or changing devices. Full restoration of every type of editable project is not guaranteed.

10

Print at actual size

  1. Open the PDF in a standard viewer and choose A4 or A3 paper to match.
  2. Set Actual size or 100%; disable fit-to-page and automatic scaling.
  3. Print the calibration page first. Measure the square: it must be 50 × 50 mm.
  4. Print the other pages. Align row R and column C labels and overlapping areas. Trim excess margins and tape sheets together without scaling.
  5. Verify the final dimensions before transferring to material. The paint-by-number Fit on one sheet option fits the picture to paper rather than preserving arbitrary dimensions.

Tips and safety

If no outline is found, use a higher-contrast photo, adjust subject boundaries or change tolerance. If PDF export fails, check dimensions, sheet count, available storage and whether processing is complete. Reduce painting detail if regions are too small. Use suitable materials and tools; children should use cutting tools only with adult supervision. Makeit generates templates; users are responsible for making, strength and safe use of the finished object.
