Privacy Policy — Tarkastus Cloud

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · Developer: Vlad Vastsenko, independent developer, Finland (EU)

This policy explains how the Tarkastus Cloud Android application handles personal data. Contact the developer at info@vladi.fi.

1. What the app does

Tarkastus Cloud creates construction inspection reports, including waterproofing inspection reports, and supports photographs, PDF export, optional account sign-in, and optional Google Drive synchronization and backups. Core report creation can be used locally without an account.

2. Data you provide

Depending on how you use the app, information may include your name, email address, company and inspector details, customer and construction-site information, report text, photographs, signatures, and PDF documents. Reports can contain personal data about other people; only enter and share information you are authorized to use.

Report content and settings are stored on your device. When you choose Google Drive features, the selected files and synchronization data are transferred to the Google Drive folders you authorize. People who have access to a shared folder may access its shared content. Private backups should be kept outside shared company folders.

3. Accounts and authentication

Optional email/password or Google sign-in uses Google Firebase Authentication. Firebase may process account identifiers, email addresses, authentication credentials or tokens, and security-related technical data to provide and protect sign-in. The developer does not need to receive your password in plain text.

4. Advertising and consent

The free app uses Google AdMob to display advertising outside report-editing screens. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, approximate location inferred from technical signals, interaction data and other information, depending on your device settings, region and consent choices. Where required, the app presents Google's consent message before requesting ads. You may revisit advertising privacy choices through the app when this option is required in your region. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy information.

5. Purposes and legal bases

We process information to provide requested report, account, backup and synchronization features; maintain security; respond to support requests; and display ads that support the free app. Under GDPR, the applicable legal bases may include performance of a contract or steps requested by you, legitimate interests in service security and support, and consent where required for advertising technologies. You may withdraw consent through available privacy controls.

6. Sharing and service providers

Data may be processed by Google services used by the app, including Firebase Authentication, Google Drive and Google AdMob. Content shared into a Google Drive folder is available according to that folder's access permissions, which you or the folder owner control. We do not sell your report contents to advertisers. Service providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Storage, retention and deletion

Local reports, photos and backups remain on your device or in the Google Drive locations you select until removed by you, subject to device and Google Drive behavior. Deleting an app or local file does not necessarily delete copies stored in Google Drive or held by other shared-folder participants. Authentication records and provider-held data are subject to their applicable retention policies.

To request deletion of an app account or personal data handled by the developer, email info@vladi.fi with the subject “Tarkastus Cloud data deletion”. We may need to verify that you control the account. You can remove local data from your device and manage Google Drive files and permissions in Google Drive. We will respond to privacy requests in accordance with applicable law, generally within one month under GDPR.

8. International processing

Google services may process data outside Finland or the European Economic Area. Applicable transfer safeguards and further details are described in Google's privacy and service documentation.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

10. Security

Use device security, protect your account credentials, and limit shared-folder access. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Users remain responsible for deciding which report information and photographs they share.

11. Children

The app is designed for professional construction-related use, not specifically for children. It is not intended to be directed to children.

12. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's services or legal requirements change. Updates will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, contact info@vladi.fi.

This policy describes the intended production configuration. Features and third-party disclosures must be reviewed against the final release before publication.
